FairPick

The random student picker that's actually fair — nobody gets picked twice until everyone has had a turn.

How to use

  1. Paste your class list below — one name per line — or tap Try a sample class.
  2. Press Pick a student. FairPick only chooses from students who haven't had a turn this round.
  3. When everyone has been picked, a new round starts automatically.
  4. Save named lists (Year 5A, Year 6B…) to reuse them next lesson — they're stored in this browser only.

Shortcuts: Space pick a student · N new round · F fullscreen
